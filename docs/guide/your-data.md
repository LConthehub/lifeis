# Your data

Life Is is built so that nobody else holds your household. There is no Life Is account and no Life Is cloud.

## Where it is kept

- **In the phone app:** in an encrypted database on the phone. The key that opens it is kept in the phone's own secure storage.
- **On a computer you run:** in an encrypted database in the folder you chose, with the key that opens it kept apart from it.

A stolen disk shows only scrambled data.

## What can leave, and when

Life Is works without the internet. A few things reach outside, and each one is **off until you turn it on** and **says exactly what it sends** before you do:

| What | Sends |
|---|---|
| Barcode look-ups | The number under the barcode, and nothing else |
| The weather | The town you chose |
| A recipe from a website | A request for that page |
| Email | Only between Life Is and your own mail provider |
| Reminders | What you choose, the ways you choose. See [Reminders](reminders.html) |
| An AI assistant | Only when you ask it a question. A model you run yourself, or a cloud one with a plain warning |

Everything that reaches outside is listed in one place: **Settings › Connections**.

## Keeping a copy

**In the phone app:** Settings › Your data makes a backup file, locked with a passphrase you choose. Keep it wherever you like. Life Is makes no copies of itself anywhere.

**On a computer you run:** your records are the data folder and the keys folder you chose when you [installed it](install.html). Copy both, together, to keep a backup. Life Is also saves a copy of the database by itself before every upgrade.

**Keep the keys with the data.** One is no use without the other.

## Sharing inside a household

Each person in a Life Is has their own things. Nothing is seen by anyone else unless it is shared: a spending category shared by name, an upkeep job given to someone.

## Taking it with you

Recipes download as a zip. The home inventory prints or saves as a PDF. A backup holds everything.
