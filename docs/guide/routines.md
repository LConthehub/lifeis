# Routines and the chore chart

A routine is a checklist for part of a day: morning, bedtime, after school, a weekly reset. It is in the **on track** section, on the **Routines** tab. Today's routines also show on the front page, where you check steps off.

## Make a routine

1. Open **Routines** and choose **+ New**. Or open **Start from a ready-made routine** and choose **Add** on one. Ready-made ones are plain steps to copy, and every part is yours to change afterward.
2. Name it, and choose **Whose is it?**
   - **Mine** shows on your front page.
   - **Everyone's** shows on every front page.
   - **A person's** shows on their front page.
   - **A household member** (someone without a sign-in, such as a child) shows on the chore chart.
   - **A pet** shows on every front page and on the pet's page. See [Routines for a pet](#routines-for-a-pet).
3. Give each **time of day** a name, such as Morning or Evening. You can have up to six. A time can have a clock time. With one, Life Is reminds you then, in the ways your reminders already go. See [Upkeep](upkeep.html#how-you-are-told).
4. Add the steps. For each step you can choose:
   - **Every day**, or **Some days of the week** (check the days).
   - **A thing it uses**, from the things you keep. On the front page, that step then has a "running low" button that puts the thing on the grocery list.
   - **A wait after this step**, such as "Wait 1 min". You see the wait when you go through the routine.
5. Press **Save**.

For a routine for a household member, you can also set the **chore points** each finished time is worth.

## Routines for a pet

A pet can have a routine, such as a dog's morning: feed, fresh water, walk. It shows on **every** person's front page, headed with the pet's name, like "George: Morning". It also shows on the pet's own page.

1. Open the pet's page (in **Pets**) and look under **Routines**. Under **Add a routine for** the pet's name, choose **Add** on **Dog, daily** or **Cat, daily**. They are plain steps to copy, and every part is yours to change. Or choose **+ Make your own**.
2. To use the pet's food, pick it under **Food** before you add. The feed steps then use it. Or open the routine with **Change** and choose **A thing it uses** on a step.
3. Anyone who signs in to this Life Is can check off a step. Each check keeps who checked it off and when, and everyone then reads it under the step, such as "Done at 7:10 AM by Sam". So nobody feeds the dog twice. Someone without a sign-in who checks off a step on your screen is kept under your name.
4. On a feed step that uses the food, **running low** puts the food on the grocery list. Life Is does not count the food down.

Each time of day shows "5 of the last 7 days". It never counts a streak.

Only the person who made the routine can change or delete it. Monthly meds and flea treatment are not routines. They are the pet's care, and Life Is reminds you when they are due. Taking a pet away takes its routines too.

## Workouts

A workout is a routine whose steps can carry numbers. Choose **+ New workout** on the **Routines** tab. It starts as yours alone, so it shows only on your front page. Choose someone else, or **Everyone's**, under **Whose is it?** to share it like any routine.

Each step can have sets and reps, a weight, minutes or a distance. Fill in only the ones you want. A step with none is a plain checklist step. You type the unit: lb or kg, miles or km. Life Is remembers it with the step.

On the front page, each numbered step says **Last time**, such as "Last time: 3 x 10 at 25 lb, Mon". The boxes start as last time was. Change a number with a tap before you check the step off, and the numbers you checked off with are kept.

Choose **Look back** on a workout to see "5 of the last 7 days", a month of dots, and the last few times of each numbered step. Life Is keeps no streaks, points or badges, and never says you missed a day.

## Measures

The **Measures** chip at the top of Routines is for numbers you want to watch over time, such as a weight or the pages you read. Choose **+ New measure**, give it a name and a unit, and add an entry with its day whenever you like. You see the latest entries as a plain list. Add a target only if you want one.

Life Is suggests nothing and judges nothing here: no "good" or "bad", no arrows, no colors for up or down.

Measures are only ever seen by you. There is no switch to share them, not even with the person in charge. They stay out of search, the front page, reminders, the assistant, voice keys and everyone else's download. They are in your own full download, and come back in with it.

## A cycle

Some routines go round over several nights, such as a skin routine with a different step each night. Choose **Does it run in a cycle?** and how many nights, from 2 to 14. Then a step can be for "Night 3 of 4". The cycle moves on only when you finish that time of day, so nights you skip do not use up a step.

## Using it

On the front page each time of day shows the steps that fall today as boxes. Check them off. **All done** checks off the rest at once, and **Start over** clears the checks.

Choose **Step by step** to do the routine one big step at a time. **Done** checks the step off and moves on. **Back** and **Skip** move without checking. If a step has a wait, **Start the timer** counts it down. The timer is only for looking at. It does not stop anything.

Checking off routines is free in the phone app, even after the trial of the paid parts.

## The chore chart

The chore chart is a sheet for the fridge. It is made from your chores in [Upkeep](upkeep.html#chores-and-children) and from routines given to household members.

1. In **Upkeep**, choose **Print a chore chart**.
2. Pick **This week** or **Next week**, and the day the week starts on, Sunday or Monday. Life Is remembers that on this device.
3. Check whose chores go on it. Choose **Everyone on one page** or **One page per child**.
4. Print it.

Each person's chores are rows. There is a box for each day a chore is due, or one box for "any day this week". The **Points** column says what each chore is worth. At the bottom is a line to write the week's points, and, if you set what a point is worth, the amount.

Life Is does not count the checks on paper. You add up the points yourself.
