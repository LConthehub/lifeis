# Routines and the chore chart

A routine is a checklist for part of a day: morning, bedtime, after school, a weekly reset. It is in the **busy** section, on the **Routines** tab. Today's routines also show on the front page, where you tick steps off.

## Make a routine

1. Open **Routines** and choose **+ New**. Or open **Start from a ready-made routine** and choose **Add** on one. Ready-made ones are plain steps to copy, and every part is yours to change afterward.
2. Name it, and choose **Whose is it?**
   - **Mine** shows on your front page.
   - **Everyone's** shows on every front page.
   - **A person's** shows on their front page.
   - **A household member** (someone without a sign-in, such as a child) shows on the chore chart.
3. Give each **time of day** a name, such as Morning or Evening. You can have up to six. A time can have a clock time. With one, Life Is reminds you then, in the ways your reminders already go. See [Upkeep](upkeep.html#how-you-are-told).
4. Add the steps. For each step you can choose:
   - **Every day**, or **Some days of the week** (check the days).
   - **A thing it uses**, from the things you keep. On the front page, that step then has a "running low" button that puts the thing on the grocery list.
   - **A wait after this step**, such as "Wait 1 min". You see the wait when you go through the routine.
5. Press **Save**.

For a routine for a household member, you can also set the **chore points** each finished time is worth.

## A cycle

Some routines go round over several nights, such as a skin routine with a different step each night. Choose **Does it run in a cycle?** and how many nights, from 2 to 14. Then a step can be for "Night 3 of 4". The cycle moves on only when you finish that time of day, so nights you skip do not use up a step.

## Using it

On the front page each time of day shows the steps that fall today as boxes. Tick them off. **Tick them all** does the rest at once, and **Start over** clears the ticks.

Choose **Go through it** to do the routine one big step at a time. **Done** ticks the step and moves on. **Back** and **Skip** move without ticking. If a step has a wait, **Start the timer** counts it down. The timer is only for looking at. It does not stop anything.

Ticking routines is free in the phone app, even after the trial of the paid parts.

## The chore chart

The chore chart is a sheet for the fridge. It is made from your chores in [Upkeep](upkeep.html#chores-and-children) and from routines given to household members.

1. In **Upkeep**, choose **Print a chore chart**.
2. Pick **This week** or **Next week**, and the day the week starts on, Sunday or Monday. Life Is remembers that on this device.
3. Check whose chores go on it. Choose **Everyone on one page** or **One page per child**.
4. Print it.

Each person's chores are rows. There is a box for each day a chore is due, or one box for "any day this week". The **Points** column says what each chore is worth. At the bottom is a line to write the week's points, and, if you set what a point is worth, the amount.

Life Is does not count the checks on paper. You add up the points yourself.
