# On your phone

The Life Is app is on its way to the app stores. This page says how it will work.

## Two ways to use the app

When the app first opens it offers three things:

- **Start on this phone.** Everything is kept on the phone, encrypted. No server, no account. This is what most people want.
- **I have a backup.** Choose the backup file from your other phone and type its passphrase. Everything comes back as it was.
- **I host my own Life Is.** The app is a window onto the Life Is on your own computer.

You can change your mind later. Nothing held in either place is touched.

## What is free

| | |
|---|---|
| **Free for good** | The kitchen (recipes, the week, groceries, the pantry), backups, and connecting the app to a Life Is you run |
| **Free to try for 7 days** | Everything else kept on the phone |
| **One payment of $14.99** | Unlocks everything else, for good. No subscription |

The 7 days start when you first add something outside the kitchen.

**When the trial ends, nothing is hidden or deleted.** Those parts become read-only: you can still see everything and make a backup.

## Reaching a Life Is at home

A Life Is on your home computer answers only that computer unless you say otherwise ([Install](install.html) shows how). Two good ways to reach it from your phone:

- **On your home network.** Let it answer other devices on your network, and open it at your computer's address while you are at home.
- **From anywhere, privately.** A private network such as Tailscale joins your phone and your computer so the phone can reach it wherever you are, without opening it to the internet.

A web address of its own works too, but then Life Is is on the public internet, behind your password.

## The camera

Scanning barcodes and stickers uses the camera. The picture is read on the device and goes nowhere. In a browser, the camera only works over https or on the computer itself.

## Short requests

A shortcut on the app's icon, Siri or Google can add to the grocery list, note some spending, or tell you what is for dinner. Anything that adds waits for a tap, so a link alone can never change anything. To use Siri with a Life Is you run, by voice and without opening the app, see [Siri and Shortcuts](siri.html).
