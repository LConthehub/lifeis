# Get started

Life Is runs in one of two places: **on your phone**, where the app keeps everything on the phone itself, or **on a computer you run at home**, which you open from any browser. Both hold the same things and look the same.

The quickest way to see it is the [demo](https://demo.lifeis.living): a made-up household you can change freely. It resets every night.

## Your first five minutes

1. **Make your account.** On a computer, the first account is made on that computer itself, at `http://localhost:8020`. That first account is the admin. Use a password of 12 or more characters and keep it somewhere safe: nobody can reset it for you.
2. **Say what Life Is should look after.** It asks once: cooking, the things you keep, projects, films and books, money. Check what you want; the rest stays out of your way. You can change this later in Settings.
3. **Look at the front page.** It shows today and the next two days, and what needs you. Each card can be hidden with **Choose what shows**.

## Three things worth doing on day one

- **Add a few recipes.** Paste the text, bring one in from a website, or take a photo of a card. Life Is keeps what you wrote exactly as you wrote it.
- **Fill one shelf.** Open **organized**, add a place such as "Garage › Tool shelf", choose **Fill this place**, and scan or type what is on it. See [Your home](home.html).
- **Add one thing that comes round.** In **Upkeep**, tap a common one, like "Change the furnace filter" or your rent. Life Is will tell you when it is due. See [Upkeep](upkeep.html).

## Finding things

Press `/` anywhere, or tap the magnifying glass, and type. One search covers recipes, things, places, books, pets, upkeep and plans. Type "kettle" and it says which shelf or box it is on.

## Sharing it with the people you live with

On a computer you run, the admin adds people under **Settings › Account**. Each person has their own sign-in and their own things. What is shared is shared by choice: a spending category, an upkeep job given to someone. Children who do not need a sign-in can still be given chores: see [Upkeep](upkeep.html).
