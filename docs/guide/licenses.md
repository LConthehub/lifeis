# Licenses and recalls

## Licenses

**Licenses** is in the **in order** section, a chip next to the kinds on the **Things** tab. It keeps the licenses and permits your household has: driver's licenses, passports, fishing and hunting licenses, a nursing license, parking permits, pet tags. For each one it holds whose it is, its number, who issued it, and when it runs out.

### Add one

1. Choose **+ Add a license**.
2. To save typing, choose **Start from a common one**: driver's license, passport, Global Entry, vehicle registration, fishing license and more. It fills in the kind, who usually issues it, and a note. Everything it fills in can be changed.
3. Fill in what it is, **Whose it is** (leave empty for the household, or type a name, a family member or a pet), the kind, the number, who issued it, the day it was issued and the day it runs out.
4. Press **Save**.

Licenses are grouped by person. When one runs out soon or has run out, a note at the top of the page says so.

### The number

The number is not shown on the page. Choose **Show number** to see it. It hides itself again after a minute. It is kept only on the license. It is never put in search, the calendar, the morning reminder or the reminder's notes.

### A reminder to renew

A license can have a reminder, and it works like any renewal in [Upkeep](upkeep.html). It shows on the calendar and in your morning reminder.

- Choose **Remind me to renew** on a license that has a day it runs out. Or turn the reminder on while you add or change the license.
- Say how long before it runs out you want to hear: days, weeks or months. Life Is suggests a usual time for each kind, and says why.
- Fill in **Term in years**, how long a renewed one lasts, and the reminder comes round again. Leave it empty for a one-time reminder.
- If you change the day it runs out, the reminder moves with it.

When you renew, update the new expiry day on the license. Until you do, it says "Renewed? Update the new expiry day."

When you remove a license that has a reminder, you choose whether to remove the reminder too or leave it in Upkeep.

Outdoor trips check your licenses against the days of the trip. See [Outdoor trips](trips.html).

## Recall alerts

Life Is can look for safety recalls on the cars you keep. It is off until you turn it on.

**What it sends.** The make, model and year of each car, and nothing else. No VIN, no name, no account, no key. It asks the U.S. traffic safety agency (NHTSA). It does this about once a week, and when you press **Check now**.

### Turn it on

1. Open **Settings**, then **Connections**, and find **Recall alerts for your cars**.
2. Read what it sends, and press **Turn on**.

A thing counts as a car when its kind has fields named Make, Model and Year, and all three are filled in. The ready-made Vehicles kind has them. The settings page lists the cars it can check, and the ones that still need a make, model or year, with a link to each.

### What you see

On a car's page, a **Recalls** card lists its open recalls, the most serious first. Each says what the problem is, what could happen, and what to do. Some are marked **Do not drive** or **Park outside**. Choose **Dismiss** on one you have dealt with or that is not yours, and **Bring it back** if you change your mind.

Recalls are matched by make, model and year, so some listed may not apply to your very car. For a certain answer, enter your VIN on NHTSA's own site. Life Is never sends the VIN.
