# The kitchen

The **tasty** section is recipes, the week, the grocery list and the pantry. In the phone app, all of it is free for good.

## Recipes

Import a recipe four ways:

- **Paste or type it.** Ingredients one to a line, steps one to a line.
- **From a website.** Give the address and Life Is reads the recipe off the page. This reaches outside, so it is off until you turn it on.
- **From a photo.** A card or a cookbook page is read on your own device. The photo goes nowhere.
- **From handwriting.** The built-in reader can't make out handwriting. If you turn on the assistant in Settings with an AI model that can look at pictures, a handwritten card can be read too: choose **It's handwritten**. With a model on your own computer, the photo doesn't leave home. With a cloud AI, the photo is sent to it and nothing else is. Either way you check the copy against the card before it is saved.

**Life Is keeps what you wrote.** It does not shorten, reword or summarize a recipe. The original text is always there.

A recipe can say which pans and dishes it needs, from the things you keep. Planning a big meal, Life Is tells you if you are short of an 8x8 dish before the day.

## The cooking view

Open a recipe and choose **Start cooking**. The recipe fills the screen: big ingredients, then the steps, each with a large box to tick. Tick a step and the next one that is not done scrolls to the middle. If you set a number of servings first, the amounts are scaled and shown beside each ingredient.

Where the phone or browser allows it, the screen stays on while the view is open. If it cannot, a note says the screen may dim, so tap now and then. The ticks are only for this cooking. Nothing is saved, and they are gone when you choose **Done cooking** or press Escape.

## The week

Put recipes on days and meals. The front page shows today's, and the calendar shows the week.

## Groceries

Fill the list from the week's meals, less what the pantry already has, or add anything by hand. Scan a package to add it. The list can be shown by store, with each thing's usual size.

## The pantry

What you have, how much, and when it should be used by. Give something a "running low at" amount and Life Is tells you when to buy more.

## Prices, coupons and savings

Note what something cost and where. Life Is shows how the price has moved and roughly what a planned meal will cost. The **Prices** tab shows what you spent on groceries by month, how the stores compare on each thing, and your stores.

On the grocery list you can add:

- **Coupons.** Give a coupon a dollar or percent amount. The list shows which thing it is for, and checking one to use on today's trip takes it off the total.
- **A store's savings book.** Type in a store's monthly savings, a line to a thing: `Coffee 4.00` or `Cereal 20%`. The list then says which things on it have a saving now. Nothing is used by itself.

Coupons about to run out appear on the front page.

## When the shopping is done

When you have checked off what you got at a store, choose **Done at** that store. Life Is fills in what the receipt should come to from the prices you paid. Type what the receipt really says, and press **Write it down**. It becomes a spending entry, so the [budget](money.html) counts it. If your bank later brings in the same charge, it is not counted twice.

- **Prices.** Change a price on the way, and the estimate follows. Each line can also have what came off it ("saved"), so the price paid and the saving are both kept.
- **A photo of the receipt.** Choose **Read a receipt photo**. The words are read on your own device, and nothing is sent to anyone. Life Is matches each line to a thing on your list and shows you what it found. Change anything that is wrong, then choose **Use these**. Lines that are not on the list are left out. You can also paste or type the receipt's words. You can keep the photo with the spending.
- **Split it.** If the receipt covers more than groceries, choose **Split this receipt**. Life Is suggests the parts from what each thing is for (the week's meals, a project's supplies, the household), and you pick a category for each. The parts must add up to the receipt.
- **Saved at the store.** The **This month** page under **balanced** shows how much you saved this month and this year with sales and coupons, by store. It is already taken off what you paid, so it is not in the spending.
- **A price came down?** If a store lets you ask for a price adjustment and a price dropped, Life Is says what to ask them for.
