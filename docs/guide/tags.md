# Tags

A tag is a short label you put on things. There is one list of tags for your household. The same tag can go on a thing, a recipe, a project, a plan, a bit of spending, a title in your collection, a pet, a job in Upkeep, a routine, a license, a date on the calendar and more.

## Put a tag on something

Open the form of what you want to tag, such as a thing, a recipe or a project, and find the Tags box. Start typing. Life Is shows tags that match, or you can make a new one by typing its name. A tag comes off with its **x**.

To tag many things at once, choose **Choose several** on a list, check the ones you want, and then use **Add a tag** or **Take a tag off**.

Most lists also have a tag filter. Pick a tag and only what has it is shown.

## Nest tags

A tag can sit inside another one: Appliances inside Kitchen. Asking for Kitchen then shows what is tagged Appliances too. When you type a tag in a form, you can write a path with `>` between the levels, such as `Kitchen > Appliances`, and each level is made for you.

## See and change your tags

Open **Settings**, find **Tags**, and choose **See and change your tags**. You see all of them as a tree, with how many things each one has.

To add one, give it a name and say what it sits inside. To change one, choose **Change** beside it, or open it and choose **Change this tag**:

- **Rename** it.
- **Put it inside** another tag, or move it back to the top.
- **Merge** it into another tag. What had this tag now has the other one, and this one goes away.
- **Delete** it. It comes off everything that has it, and tags inside it move up one level. Nothing else is deleted.

A tag's own page lists what has it, grouped by kind, and what was spent with it this month, this year and all time. Spending counts the tag and the tags inside it, each entry once.

## Search

Search can look in tags too. Choose Tags in the filter to find one by name.
