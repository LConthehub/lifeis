The kitchen

The tasty section is recipes, the week, the grocery list and the pantry. In the phone app, all of it is free for good.

Recipes

Import a recipe four ways:

  • Paste or type it. Ingredients one to a line, steps one to a line.
  • From a website. Give the address and Life Is reads the recipe off the page. This reaches outside, so it is off until you turn it on.
  • From a photo. A card or a cookbook page is read on your own device. The photo goes nowhere.
  • From handwriting. The built-in reader can't make out handwriting. If you turn on the assistant in Settings with an AI model that can look at pictures, a handwritten card can be read too: choose It's handwritten. With a model on your own computer, the photo doesn't leave home. With a cloud AI, the photo is sent to it and nothing else is. Either way you check the copy against the card before it is saved.

Life Is keeps what you wrote. It does not shorten, reword or summarise a recipe. The original text is always there.

A recipe can say which pans and dishes it needs, from the things you keep. Planning a big meal, Life Is tells you if you are short of an 8x8 dish before the day.

The week

Put recipes on days and meals. The front page shows today's, and the calendar shows the week.

Groceries

Fill the list from the week's meals, less what the pantry already has, or add anything by hand. Scan a package to add it. The list can be shown by store, with each thing's usual size.

The pantry

What you have, how much, and when it should be used by. Give something a "running low at" amount and Life Is tells you when to buy more.

Prices and coupons

Note what something cost and where. Life Is shows how the price has moved and roughly what a planned meal will cost. Coupons about to run out appear on the front page.
