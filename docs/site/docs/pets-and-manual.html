Pets and the house manual

Pets

Each pet has a profile: what they are, their breed and birthday, their vet with a number you can tap to call, their microchip number, what they eat, and notes such as allergies. Add a photo, and note their weight from time to time: Life Is shows the change since last time.

Care

A pet's care is upkeep that belongs to them. Pick from what suits their kind (heartworm prevention for a dog, the litter box for a cat) or add anything else. Say when it was last done and Life Is knows when it is next due.

Because care is upkeep, it is in your morning reminder and on the calendar, and each time it was done is kept. See Upkeep.

The house manual

The house manual is what someone else needs to know to look after your home: a sitter, a guest, family, or you on the day something goes wrong.

It has four parts, each with ideas to start from:

  • In an emergency: where the water and gas shut off, the breaker box
  • Who to call: the plumber, a neighbor with a key
  • How the house works: the Wi-Fi, the thermostat, the alarm
  • Day to day: trash days, plants, bedtimes

An entry can say where a thing is, using the places you already keep, and carry a phone number to tap. Your pets appear by themselves, with their food and vet.

Printing and sharing

Print it and put it in a drawer.

Or share it by a link, for a sitter. The link opens the manual on their phone with no sign-in. You choose how long it works: a day, a week, or until you turn it off. Making a new link stops the old one.

Mark an entry private, such as an alarm code, and it is never on the shared link.

The shared page is plain: nothing on it can be changed, and it shows nothing else of your Life Is.
