Your data

Nobody else holds your household. There is no Life Is account and no Life Is cloud.

Where it is kept

  • In the phone app: in an encrypted database on the phone. The key that opens it is kept in the phone's own secure storage.
  • On a computer you run: in an encrypted database in the folder you chose, with the key kept apart from it.

A stolen disk shows only scrambled data.

What can leave, and when

Life Is works without the internet. A few things reach outside, and each one is off until you turn it on and says exactly what it sends before you do:

WhatSends
Barcode look-upsThe number under the barcode, and nothing else
The weatherThe town you chose
A recipe from a websiteA request for that page
EmailOnly between Life Is and your own mail provider
RemindersWhat you choose, the ways you choose. See Upkeep
An AI assistantOnly when you ask it a question. A model you run yourself, or a cloud one with a plain warning

All of them are listed in one place: Settings › Connections.

Keeping a copy

In the phone app: Settings › Your data makes a backup file, locked with a passphrase you choose. Keep it wherever you like. Life Is makes no copies of itself anywhere.

On a computer you run: copy the data folder and the keys folder you chose when you installed it. Keep both together: one is no use without the other. Life Is also saves a copy of the database by itself before every upgrade.

Taking it with you

Recipes download as a zip. The home inventory prints or saves as a PDF. A backup holds everything.

Sharing inside a household

See Get started. Nothing is seen by anyone else unless it is shared.
