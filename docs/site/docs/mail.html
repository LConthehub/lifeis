Mail

Life Is can show your inbox, so you can read and answer mail without leaving it. This is optional. It is off until you add an account.

This works on a Life Is you run yourself, in a browser or in the phone app signed in to it. A Life Is that is not set up to reach outside says so, and mail cannot be turned on.

What it sends

Life Is signs in to your mail provider from your own computer, with the address and password you give it. It reads your inbox as you look. Nothing goes anywhere else. Life Is does not keep your mail: each message is read from your provider when you open it.

The password is stored locked on your computer. If you remove the account, Life Is forgets the password.

Add an account

  1. Open Settings, then Email, and choose + Add an email account.
  2. Type your address and your password. Some providers need an app password instead of your everyday one. When you type your address, Life Is shows a note if yours is one of those:

- Gmail: turn on 2-step verification in your Google account, make an app password, and use that here. - Yahoo: make an app password in your account's security settings. - Fastmail: make an app password with access to mail. - iCloud: make an app-specific password at account.apple.com, under Sign-In and Security. - Outlook: Microsoft has been switching off password sign-in for mail programs. If it is refused, Outlook cannot be connected yet. - Proton Mail: only through its Bridge app, running on the same computer as Life Is. Use the server names, ports and password Bridge shows, and check On my own computer or network.

  1. Add your name as replies should show it, if you like.
  2. For the providers above, the servers fill in by themselves. For any other, choose Show the server details and fill in the incoming (IMAP) and outgoing (SMTP) servers and their ports. Any provider that lets mail programs in will work.
  3. Press Sign in and add. The account is saved only if signing in works.

Reading and answering

On the front page, the Email card shows how many messages are unread and the newest ones. Choose Inbox to open the inbox. If you have more than one account, pick it from the list.

  • Opening a message does not mark it read. Choose Mark as read or Mark as unread yourself.
  • Attachments stay with your mail provider. Life Is lists their names. Open them in your mail program.
  • Choose Reply, or Reply to everyone when there are other people on it. You write plain words, and the message you are answering is quoted beneath them. Press Send. It goes out through your provider, from your address.

Life Is shows the plain words of a message. It does not start new messages, and it does not file or delete them.

To hide the Email card from the front page, use Customize.

Reminders by email

Mail is separate from the morning reminder by email. That one sends to your own address from your own account. See Upkeep.
