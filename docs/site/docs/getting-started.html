Get started

Life Is runs on your phone, where the app keeps everything on the phone itself, or on a computer you run at home, which you open from any browser. Both hold the same things and look the same.

To see it first, try the demo: a made-up household you can change freely. It resets every night.

Your first five minutes

  1. Make your account. On a computer, make the first account on that computer itself, at http://localhost:8020. That first account is the admin. Use a password of 12 or more characters and keep it somewhere safe: nobody can reset it for you.
  2. Say what Life Is should look after. Check what you want: cooking, the things you keep, projects, films and books, money. The rest stays out of your way. You can change this later in Settings.
  3. Look at the front page. It shows today and the next two days, and what needs you. Hide any card with Choose what shows.

Three things worth doing on day one

  • Add a few recipes. Paste the text, bring one in from a website, or take a photo of a card. Life Is keeps what you wrote exactly as you wrote it.
  • Fill one shelf. Open organized, add a place such as "Garage › Tool shelf", choose Fill this place, and scan or type what is on it. See Your home.
  • Add one thing that comes round. In Upkeep, tap a common one, like "Change the furnace filter" or your rent. Life Is tells you when it is due. See Upkeep.

Finding things

Press / anywhere, or tap the magnifying glass, and type. One search covers recipes, things, places, books, pets, upkeep and plans. Type "kettle" and it says which shelf or box it is on.

Sharing it with the people you live with

On a computer you run, the admin adds people under Settings › Account. Each person has their own sign-in and their own things. What is shared is shared by choice: a spending category, an upkeep job given to someone. Children without a sign-in can still be given chores: see Upkeep.
