Get started

Life Is runs on your phone, where the app keeps everything on the phone itself, or on a computer you run at home, which you open from any browser. Both hold the same things and look the same. Most people need no server: start on your phone, and move to a server later with a backup file.

To see it first, try the demo: a made-up household you can change freely. It resets every night.

Your first five minutes

  1. Make your account. On a computer, make the first account on that computer itself, at http://localhost:8020. That first account is the admin. Use a password of 12 or more characters and keep it somewhere safe: nobody can reset it for you.
  2. Say what bugs you most at home. Pick one: meals, clutter, bills, the calendar, chores, or keeping track of things. Life Is starts there, and the front page opens on that card. You can skip it.
  3. Say what Life Is should look after. Check what you want: cooking, the things you keep, projects, films and books, money. The rest stays out of your way. You can change this later in Settings.
  4. Look at the front page. It shows the next three days, starting today, and what needs you. A Start here card lists three to five small steps, about two minutes each. Each one ticks itself off when you do it. Hide the card with Hide this.

The pages start with a few made-up examples in the parts you picked. Each says Example. Tap Clear the examples to take them all away. Anything you added yourself stays.

If you set up reminders, a short question comes each Monday: what do you want to get done this week? After two weeks it asks once whether to keep it.

Three things worth doing on day one

  • Add a few recipes. Paste the text, bring one in from a website, or take a photo of a card. Life Is keeps what you wrote exactly as you wrote it.
  • Fill one shelf. Open tidy, add a place such as "Garage › Tool shelf", choose Fill this place, and scan or type what is on it. See Your home.
  • Add one thing that comes round. In Upkeep, tap a common one, like "Change the furnace filter" or your rent. Life Is tells you when it is due. See Upkeep.

Getting around

Life Is reads as a sentence: "Life is" and then a word. There are five words, and each is a part of the app:

WordWhat is in it
tastyRecipes, the week, groceries, the pantry, prices. See The kitchen.
tidyThings, places, upkeep, pets, licenses and the House Manual. See Your home, Upkeep and Licenses and recalls.
balancedMoney: this month, the year, goals, and income and bills. See Money and budget.
busyThe calendar, routines, projects, plans, holidays, trips and activities. See Routines, Plans and projects, Holidays and Outdoor trips.
entertainingYour collection of books, films, shows, music and games, your lists, and your connections to servers. See Films, music and books.

On a computer or a tablet, the word at the top of the page is a drop-down. Tap it to see "Life is ..." with home and the five words. Tap the heart and "Life is" to go home.

On a phone, the same words are in a bar along the bottom of the screen, each with a small picture over it. It stays on screen so a thumb can reach it.

Under the top bar, each part has its own tabs, such as Recipes, Week and Groceries under tasty. When there are too many to fit, the tabs wrap onto a second line. Settings is at the top right.

Only the parts you chose in step 2 above show up. You can change that in Settings.

Finding things

Press / anywhere, or tap the magnifying glass, and type. One search covers recipes, things, places, books, pets, upkeep and plans. Type "kettle" and it says which shelf or box it is on.

Sharing it with the people you live with

On a computer you run, the admin adds people under Settings › Account & sign-in. Each person has their own sign-in and their own things. What is shared is shared by choice: a spending category, an upkeep job given to someone. Children without a sign-in can still be given chores: see Upkeep.
