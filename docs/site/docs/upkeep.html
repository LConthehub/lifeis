Upkeep

Upkeep (in tidy) is one list of everything that comes round again, in four kinds.

KindForWhen you have done it
JobsThe furnace filter, the gutters, the oil changeDone
BillsRent, electricity, internetPaid
RenewalsCar registration, insurance, a passportRenewed
ChoresTrash, dishes, tidying a roomDone, with points

How often

A job comes round by the calendar ("every 3 months"), by a reading ("every 5,000 miles", "every 100 hours"), or both, whichever comes first.

A reading is kept on the thing it is read from. Note the car's miles once and every job for the car knows.

What a job needs

A job can list the tools it needs and the supplies it uses up, all from the things you keep. Its page shows where each one is, so you can gather them.

When you mark a job done:

  • each tool's history says it was used for the job
  • supplies come out of what you have, and anything now running low goes on the grocery list
  • the cost can go into the month's spending

Bills and renewals

A bill stays on its date. Rent paid four days late is still due on the 1st next month.

Give a bill its usual amount and Life Is tells you what your bills and renewals come to in a month, with yearly ones spread over the year. A bill that pays itself can be marked so: it becomes a reminder that money is about to leave.

In the budget

The budget under balanced reads your bills from here, so there is one list. Add a bill from Income and bills and it is added to Upkeep too; open it there to change it or mark it paid. The Money guide covers the rest of the budget.

Chores, and children

A chore is worth points to whoever does it. Chores this week counts them for everyone.

People without a sign-in of their own can be added by name alone, under Chores. Give them chores, and choose who did one when you mark it done. Set what a point is worth and the board shows what each has earned.

For the fridge, choose Print a chore chart. It makes a sheet for this week or next, with each person's chores and a box to check for each day one is due. You pick whose chores go on it, and whether everyone shares a page or each child gets one. Points and what they are worth are on it for you to add up. Routines given to a household member show on it too. See Routines and the chore chart.

Sharing a job

A job can be given to someone else in your Life Is. They see it in their own Upkeep and can mark it done. Only its owner can change it.

What comes due

Due jobs are on the calendar and the front page. Each thing lists the upkeep it is part of.

You are also told. Each morning, at a time you pick, Life Is works out what needs you:

  • upkeep that is late, due, or coming up in the next few days
  • things running low, and food to use soon
  • birthdays, plans starting, and today's events
  • coupons about to run out, and things lent out for a long time
  • warranties about to end

It goes once a day, and only on days there is something to say. The time you pick is in your own time zone, wherever your Life Is runs.

Set it up under Settings › Reminders. That page shows a preview of this morning's digest, and each way has a Send one now button so you can see it work. Check or uncheck each kind of reminder to turn off what you do not want to hear about.

How you are told

Every way is off until you turn it on, and says what it sends.

WayWhat it sends, and to whom
On this phoneMade on the phone from what is kept on it. Nothing leaves the phone.
Email to yourselfSent from your own connected email account to that same address. Nobody new is involved.
Your browserLocked so only your browser can read it, carried by your browser maker's push service. They see that a message went, not what it says.
ntfyTo the ntfy server you choose. ntfy messages are not locked, so by default it says only how many things there are, not what they are.
