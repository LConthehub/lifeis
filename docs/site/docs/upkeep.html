Upkeep

Upkeep (in organized) is one list of everything that comes round again, in four kinds.

KindForWhen you have done it
JobsThe furnace filter, the gutters, the oil changeDone
BillsRent, electricity, internetPaid
RenewalsCar registration, insurance, a passportRenewed
ChoresTrash, dishes, tidying a roomDone, with points

How often

A job comes round by the calendar ("every 3 months"), by a reading ("every 5,000 miles", "every 100 hours"), or both, whichever comes first.

A reading is kept on the thing it is read from. Note the car's miles once and every job for the car knows.

What a job needs

A job can list the tools it needs and the supplies it uses up, all from the things you keep. Its page shows where each one is, so you can gather them.

When you mark a job done:

  • each tool's history says it was used for the job
  • supplies come out of what you have, and anything now running low goes on the grocery list
  • the cost can go into the month's spending

Bills and renewals

A bill stays on its date. Rent paid four days late is still due on the 1st next month.

Give a bill its usual amount and Life Is tells you what your bills and renewals come to in a month, with yearly ones spread over the year. A bill that pays itself can be marked so: it becomes a reminder that money is about to leave.

Chores, and children

A chore is worth points to whoever does it. Chores this week counts them for everyone.

People without a sign-in of their own can be added by name alone, under Chores. Give them chores, and choose who did one when you mark it done. Set what a point is worth and the board shows what each has earned.

Sharing a job

A job can be given to someone else in your Life Is. They see it in their own Upkeep and can mark it done. Only its owner can change it.

Where it shows

Due jobs are on the calendar and the front page, and in your morning reminder. Each thing lists the upkeep it is part of.
