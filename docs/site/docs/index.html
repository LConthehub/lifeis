Guides

How Life Is works, part by part. New here? Start with the first one, or try the demo and come back when you have a question.

Get startedMake your account, say what Life Is should look after, and do the three things worth doing on day one. Install with DockerPick your options and get a ready setup file to copy. Your homePlaces, things, records for your insurer, filling a shelf by scanning, pets, and the House Manual. UpkeepJobs, bills, renewals and chores, and how Life Is tells you what is due. Licenses and recallsKeep licenses, passports and permits with their expiry days, get a reminder to renew, and check your cars for safety recalls. The kitchenRecipes kept as you wrote them, the week's meals, groceries, the pantry and what things cost. Money and budgetSpending by category, income and bills, goals, and the year at a glance. Routines and the chore chartChecklists for parts of the day, workouts with numbers, your own measures, and a printed chore chart for the fridge. Plans and projectsPlans for a day or a trip, with food, things and checklists, and projects with what they use, what they cost and the time on them. HolidaysHolidays that come back every year, planned from last year's: the dinner countdown, guests, cards, gifts, decorations and budget. Outdoor tripsCamping, fishing and hunting trips that come back every year, each started from last year's. TagsOne list of tags for the whole household, nested if you like, on almost everything you keep. Films, music and booksEverything you own on every format, in one list. Shelves, servers, Kindle and the rest. MailRead and answer your email inside Life Is, on a Life Is you run. Optional, off until you add an account. Reading your bankBring spending in from your bank accounts through SimpleFIN, read only, on a Life Is you run. On your phoneThe app, what is free, the trial, and reaching a Life Is you run at home. Siri and ShortcutsAsk your iPhone for the grocery list, dinner and your day, and add to them by voice, with a personal key. Your dataWhere everything is kept, how it is locked, what can leave, and how to keep a copy. TroubleshootingWhen something does not open, scan or arrive.
