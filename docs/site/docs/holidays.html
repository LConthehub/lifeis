Holidays

A holiday is a plan that comes back each year. Each year gets its own copy, started from last year's, so you do not begin from nothing. Life Is suggests no holidays. You add the ones you keep.

Holidays are in the on track section, on the Plans tab. Choose the Holidays chip at the top.

Add a holiday

  1. Choose + Add a holiday and give it a name, such as Thanksgiving.
  2. Under Its day, pick the rule:

- A weekday of a month, such as the fourth Thursday of November. Choose which (first to fourth, or last), the weekday and the month. - The same date every year, such as December 25. Choose the month and the day. - I'll set it each year. For a day that moves, such as Easter. You type this year's day now, and Life Is asks for next year's day when this year is finished.

  1. Say Start planning this many weeks ahead. It starts at 6. During those weeks the holiday shows on your front page, with what to do next.
  2. Press Add it.

From a date you already have

If you keep a birthday, an anniversary or another yearly date on the calendar, the Holidays page offers Make a holiday beside it. It becomes a holiday with the same date every year. The date stays on the calendar.

Plan a year

Open the holiday and start this year's. It starts from last year's: the same guests, cards, decorations and countdown steps, with nothing checked off. What broke or ran out last year goes on a To buy for this year list. If you left yourself a note last year, you see it at the top.

Each year is its own plan. Use the years along the top to look back. Past years stay.

Under The day, set:

  • The day, if it moved.
  • Dinner is at, a time.
  • Frozen turkey, in pounds, if you have one.
  • Budget, a target in dollars.
  • Put the decorations up on and Take them down on. You get a reminder the morning of each of those days if something is left to do.

The dinner countdown

Once you set when dinner is, Life Is works backward from it and lists what to do and when. It includes:

  • each recipe's prep reminders, such as marinating the night before
  • each recipe's cooking time, so you know when to start it
  • a thaw for a frozen turkey
  • steps of your own

For a frozen turkey, Life Is says how many days it needs in the refrigerator: 24 hours for each 4 pounds, rounded up to be safe. It shows where that rule comes from.

To add a step of your own, such as "Make the gravy", pick when: the night before or the morning of (with a time), or some hours before dinner. Check steps off as you do them. A step that is past its time says late.

The menu

Add recipes from your kitchen to the menu. The full plan, with its checklist and photos, is a link away. See Plans and projects.

Who came, and cards

  • Who came. Add who is invited, and check each one off as they come. Pick people from your household or type a name. Next year starts with the same list.
  • Cards. Add who gets a card, and check each one off as you send it.

Gifts

Write a gift idea down whenever it comes, all year. Each has a person and an idea, and moves through An idea, Bought, Wrapped and Given. When it is bought, add what it cost. Ideas you did not use carry over to next year, and a person's list shows what you gave them in other years.

A gift for someone who signs in to Life Is is hidden from them until the day has passed. After that, a Given to you part on the Holidays page shows what they were given.

Decorations

Under Decorations: put up, add each decoration and where it goes: a room or a spot. A decoration can be one of your things, or something you name, kept in a box (a place). Take a photo of each spot. Next year the list shows last year's photo beside the spot, so you can put it back the same way. Check each decoration off as it goes up.

Under Take down, check each decoration off as it comes down and again when it is back in its box. Mark one Broke or Ran out and it lands on next year's shopping list. When it is all done, choose Everything is down and boxed.

Budget

The budget card shows what was spent against your target. It counts spending tagged with the holiday's name in the year around the day. A gift marked as bought, with its cost, is counted too. See Tags.

Goal and cost

Each year has a Goal and cost card. It adds up what Life Is already knows: the menu priced from your store prices and the gifts that have a price. You can type lines of your own too. Anything with no price says so, like "3 things have no price yet". Start a goal for the total and the budget shows what to set aside each month. A gift for someone who signs in to Life Is is left out of what they see, until the day has passed.

Finish the year

Under How it went, write a note on how it went, and a note for next year, such as "Less stuffing. Start the pie the day before." Choose Finish this year. Your note is kept for next year, and next year is started from this one. Nothing is deleted.

To stop keeping a holiday, choose Stop keeping this holiday. Each of its years stays as a plan to look back on.
