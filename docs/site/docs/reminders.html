Reminders

Each morning, at a time you pick, Life Is works out what needs you:

  • upkeep that is late, due, or coming up in the next few days
  • things running low, and food to use soon
  • birthdays, plans starting, and today's events
  • coupons about to run out, and things lent out for a long time
  • warranties about to end

It goes once a day, and only on days there is something to say.

Set it up under Settings › Reminders. That page shows a preview of this morning's digest, and each way has a Send one now button so you can see it work.

The ways it can reach you

Every way is off until you turn it on, and says what it sends.

WayWhat it sends, and to whom
On this phoneMade on the phone from what is kept on it. Nothing leaves the phone.
Email to yourselfSent from your own connected email account to that same address. Nobody new is involved.
Your browserLocked so only your browser can read it, carried by your browser maker's push service. They see that a message went, not what it says.
ntfyTo the ntfy server you choose. ntfy messages are not locked, so by default it says only how many things there are, not what they are.

Choosing what it covers

Tick or untick each kind of reminder. Turn off what you do not want to hear about.

Time zones

The time you pick is in your own time zone, wherever your Life Is runs.
