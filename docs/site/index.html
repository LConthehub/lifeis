What it does

Five parts, and each finishes the sentence "Life is…". Use one, or all of them. They know about each other: a tool you scan into the garage is the same tool a job needs and a project uses.

🍳

Life is tasty.

Food. Recipes kept exactly as you wrote them, the meals for the week, a grocery list that knows your pantry, and what things cost at each store.

🏠

Life is tidy.

Home. Everything you own, room by room, with its receipt, manual and warranty, and a note when you sell it or give it away. Upkeep that comes due: the furnace filter, the oil change, the bills, the chores. Your pets, your licenses and a House Manual to hand a sitter.

💵

Life is balanced.

Money. A plan for the month by category, private unless you share it. Your income, your bills, goals and the whole year in one view.

🧵

Life is busy.

Everything with a date. One calendar for your dates, the week's meals and what is due. Routines, projects with what they use and the time on them, plans, holidays that come back each year, and camping, fishing and hunting trips.

📚

Life is entertaining.

Media. Books, films, music and games on every format, on the shelf or on your own server. Who has borrowed what, and what you watched or read and when.

One search, and reminders that reach you

Type "kettle" and Life Is says which box it's in. Type a serial number, a recipe, a pet's name. Each morning it tells you what needs you today, the ways you choose, and only on days there is something to say.

Your data stays yours

Life Is is built so that nobody else holds your household.

On your device, encrypted

Everything is kept in an encrypted database on your phone, or on a computer you run yourself. There is no Life Is account and no Life Is cloud.

Nothing leaves unless you say

Looking up a barcode, the weather, email: each is off until you turn it on, and each says exactly what it sends before you do.

No ads, no tracking

This website loads nothing from anywhere else and sets no cookies. Neither does the app. Read the privacy policy: it's short.

One fair price

No subscription, and nothing you entered is ever held hostage.
