What it does

Six parts, and each finishes the sentence "Life is…". Use one, or all of them. They know about each other: a tool you scan into the garage is the same tool a job needs and a project uses.

🍳

Life is eating.

Recipes kept exactly as you wrote them, the meals for the week, a grocery list that knows your pantry, and what things cost at each store.

🏠

Life is keeping.

Everything you own, room by room, with its receipt, manual and warranty. Upkeep that comes due: the furnace filter, the oil change, the bills, the chores. Care for your pets. And a house manual to hand a sitter.

💵

Life is spending.

A plan for the month by category, private unless you share it. Bills you pay go straight in.

📅

Life is planning.

One calendar for your dates, the meals for the week and what is due. Plans for a trip or a party, with their food, their packing and their photos.

🧵

Life is making.

Projects with what they use, what they cost and the time on them, tied to the things and tools you already have.

📚

Life is entertaining.

Books, films, music and games on every format, on the shelf or on your own server. Who has borrowed what, and what you watched or read and when.

One search, and reminders that reach you

Type "kettle" and Life Is says which box it's in. Type a serial number, a recipe, a pet's name. Each morning it tells you what needs you today, the ways you choose, and only on days there is something to say.

Your data stays yours

Life Is is built so that nobody else holds your household.

On your device, encrypted

Everything is kept in an encrypted database on your phone, or on a computer you run yourself. There is no Life Is account and no Life Is cloud.

Nothing leaves unless you say

Looking up a barcode, the weather, email: each is off until you turn it on, and each says exactly what it sends before you do.

No ads, no tracking

This website loads nothing from anywhere else and sets no cookies. Neither does the app. Read the privacy policy: it's short.
