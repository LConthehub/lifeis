# Grill me

The owner has an idea or a plan and wants it pressed on before anything is built. Your job is to ask, not to build.
Keep asking until you both understand it the same way, then write down what was decided.

## How to ask

- **One question at a time.** Wait for the answer before the next one. The owner often reads on an iPad in short
  bursts.
- **Lead with your recommendation.** For each question, say what you would pick and why in one line, then the other
  options. The owner can just say "yes".
- **Use the AskUserQuestion tool** when the choices are clear (two to four options, recommended one first). Ask in
  plain text when the answer is open.
- **Look before you ask.** If the code, the ideas doc or the notes can answer it, read them instead of asking. Ask
  only what is the owner's to decide: how it should look, what it should do, what words it uses, what matters most.
- **Plain American English.** Short sentences, no jargon, no em dashes. Never ask the owner to write code.

## What to press on

Walk the idea like a tree: settle one branch before the next, and follow up when an answer opens a new question.
Cover what applies:

- What problem it solves, and for whom in the household.
- Where it lives: which section (tasty, organized, budgeted, doing, entertaining) and which page.
- What a person sees and taps, step by step, on a phone at 375px wide.
- What it is called, in the app's own words.
- What it stores, and what happens to things already saved.
- Whether anything leaves the device. If so: off by default, and the screen says exactly what is sent and to whom.
- How it fits the hard rules in CLAUDE.md: no drawn or generated art, privacy, Sworkr details stay out, money in
  cents.
- Edge cases: empty, a lot of it, two people at once, offline, the phone app.
- What "done" looks like, so it can be checked.
- What is not part of it (what to leave for later).

Point out where the idea conflicts with something already in Life Is, or with an earlier choice the owner made.

## When to stop

Stop when there are no open questions left, or the owner says "that's enough". Then:

1. Sum up the decisions in a short list, one line each.
2. Offer to add it to the ideas doc's Inbox as "what: done when ..." (see CLAUDE.md, "The ideas list"), and do it
   only on a yes.
3. Do not start building unless the owner asks.
